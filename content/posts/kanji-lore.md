Visual language is as much about what you say as how you say it. In this portfolio, I've used Kanji not just for the "High-Pulse Noir" aesthetic, but to mirror the duality of my career: the **Book Smart** rigor of Physics/CS and the **Street Smart** intuition of the Bangalore night shift.

Here is the breakdown of the characters and the "Lore" behind them.

---

## 軌跡 (Kiseki) — Trajectory
**"Path" or "Locus."**
Used in the About section. It represents the non-linear move from a "Tier-100" college to driving growth for global companies. A trajectory isn't a straight line; it's a series of points mapped over time. This is my map.

## 夜勤 (Yakin) — Night Shift
**"After-hours Work."**
The title for the mixology and DJing chapter. It represents the labor that happens while the world sleeps—the foundation of the "Triple Threat" identity.

## 調奏 (Chousou) — Mixology
**"Harmonizing/Tuning."**
I used this for the Mixology card. It literally means "to tune and play," representing the art of harmonizing flavors and reading the energy of a room behind a bar.

## 音色 (Neiro) — Sound Character
**"Tone Color" or "Timbre."**
Used for DJing. It describes the unique quality of a sound. In curation, it’s about finding the "color" that connects a diverse crowd.

## 無 (Mu) — Void / Nothingness
**"Nothingness."**
Displayed alongside the "Saying no is saving time" card. It represents the Zen-like clarity that comes from intentional subtraction. Growth is often about what you *don't* do.

## 絆 (Kizuna) — Bond
**"Connection."**
Found in the Contact section. It represents the depth of collaboration I aim for—not just "networking," but creating lasting dents in the universe together.

---

## The Floating Symbols (Bleach Lore)
The background of the Hero section is alive with kinetic symbols from the *Bleach* universe:
- **魂 (Kon)**: Soul.
- **霊 (Rei)**: Spirit.
- **斬 (Zan)**: To Slay / Cut through noise.
- **道 (Dou)**: The Way.
- **間 (Ma)**: The space between notes/intervals.

---

Every character here is a reminder that the best work happens at the intersection of discipline and chaos. ⛩️🌌
